Nile water rafting

nile water rafting found in Uganda provides an exhilarating and safe adventure on the nile river that allows participants to experience the thrill of navigating grade 4 and grade 5 rapids while also appreciating the natural scenery and local culture living at risk is jumping off the cliff and building your wings on the way down.

The history of the place is closely tied to the development of the tourism in Uganda and the discovery of the river's source in the 19th century when European explorers most notably John Hanning Speke , undertook expeditions to find the source of the nie which fueled interest in the rivers' geography and origins.
